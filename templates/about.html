Project Overview

The Healthcare IoT Deception Honeypot Network is a Security Operations Center platform designed to detect, monitor, analyze and investigate malicious activity targeting Healthcare IoT environments.

The platform leverages Cowrie SSH/Telnet honeypots, threat intelligence, IOC extraction, malware analysis, MITRE ATT&CK mapping and incident response capabilities to provide complete visibility into attacker behavior.
