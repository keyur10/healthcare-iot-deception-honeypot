Project Overview

This platform provides real-time monitoring, threat intelligence collection, attack analysis, and visualization of malicious activity targeting Healthcare IoT infrastructure.

The system leverages a Cowrie SSH/Telnet honeypot to capture attacker behavior, credentials, and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), helping security analysts identify emerging threats.
